Scanella is a document scanner for iPhone. Pages you capture stay on this device. There is no account and no cloud.

Point the camera at a page. Scanella finds the edges, lets you crop and rotate, then enhance the scan. Export a PDF to Files, share it, or save the pages to Photos. Folders, Trash, OCR search, ID cards and an optional app lock are built in.

What Scanella does

The same tools that are in the iPhone app: capture, clean up, export, and keep everything local.

Nothing is uploaded

No Scanella account, no analytics, no cloud. Scans, PDFs and settings stay on the phone.

Edge detect and capture

Live edges on the page, then crop and rotate. Auto-capture fires once the sheet is steady.

Enhance

Filters, brightness and contrast after crop. Originals are kept so you can change the look later.

PDF export

Fit, A4 or Letter. Good or smaller file. Optional password. Save to Files or share from the scan.

Folders, merge, split

Group scans, merge several into one, or split a document. Trash keeps deletes for 30 days.

OCR search and ID cards

Search extracted text on the device. ID card mode captures front and back as one two-page scan.

App lock

Optional PIN, and Face ID when the phone offers it. Unlock stays on the device.
